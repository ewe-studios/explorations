colorfigures Documentation

Colorfigures: a Claude skill (by April Zhu, MIT) that draws hand-inked, flat-coloured isometric figures of everyday objects as one HTML page. Real 3D bodies shaded in flat light, heavy ink silhouette, flat blue cast shadow, warm paper. Drag to turn all the way round; See-through reveals the working mechanism inside with labelled parts. Builds on iso-glow.
