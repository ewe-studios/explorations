cinetic (cinema plus kinetic) is an Agent Skill that makes a coding agent direct and render a short motion film from code. The two main products are the launch film and the update video. It also covers product videos, feature loops, logo stings, and UI walkthroughs. The skill does not supply animation components. It supplies a production system: a script contract, a scene bank, a direction deck, a music pipeline, a sound-effect kit, gates, and a review pass.

2. Scope

This package covers the skill as a production system. It covers these areas:

  • The SKILL.md contract: the eleven standards, the hard bans, the time plan,

the eight-step path, the format table, and the craft rules.

  • The direction system: scripts/deck.py, the direction assets, and the idea

documents.

  • The scaffold and template system: scripts/new-film.sh, scripts/new-film.mjs,

and the eight template folders.

  • The scene bank and the deal: assets/remotion-starter/src/bank.ts,

src/deal.ts, src/dealt.ts, and scripts/film-diff.mjs.

  • The copy contract: scripts/copy-check.py and its rules.
  • The picture checks: scripts/lint-film.mjs, scripts/grid-check.ts,

scripts/forensics.py, and scripts/look-audit.py.

  • The sound pipeline: scripts/audio/ (fourteen files), scripts/music.mjs,

scripts/check-sync.py, and earshot.

  • The render and delivery path: scripts/render.sh, scripts/frames.mjs,

scripts/deliver.sh, and scripts/av-audit.py.

  • The review loop and the six critic files.
  • The evaluation record in docs/evaluation.md and evals/evals.json.

It does not render a film. It does not run a checker against a real film. It does not fetch a music track, a photograph, or a logo. It does not call earshot. No network request is made.

4. Constraints

  • The source is read only. No skill file is modified.
  • No render, gate, checker, installer, fetch, or test is run.
  • No network request is made.
  • Prose uses Aerospace and Defence Simplified Technical English, issue 100

(ASD-STE100). One idea per sentence. Thirty words maximum. Expand every abbreviation at first use in each file.

  • Prose avoids marketing language, em dashes, "simply", "just", and "obviously".
  • A value that the source does not define is recorded as not defined.
  • The pin is f4bbfc8. Runtime behaviour is marked as not verified.
  • A quoted line must match the source byte for byte.

9. Limits

  • Static reading only. No film, gate, render, or fetch was run.
  • The evaluation results in docs/evaluation.md are the project's own report.

The exploration repeats them and does not confirm them.

  • The seven evaluation briefs are documents. Their graded outcomes are not

reproduced here.

  • The gates depend on a working Remotion or HyperFrames project, ffmpeg, Python

packages, Chromium, and earshot. None of these was installed or run.

  • The skill makes network requests for music, photographs, and logos in normal

use. No such request was made here.

  • The 964 tracked files include 265 test files. A file count is not a measure of

the skill text.

10. Corrections and limits found during research

The inventory matched: pin f4bbfc8, 964 tracked files, MIT licence, version 1.5.1. The following findings qualify the first reading.

  1. The skill is not the whole repository. 939 of the 964 tracked files are

under skills/cinetic/. The root files, docs, evals, and the plugin manifests make up the rest.

  1. Most script files are tests. 265 of the 334 script files are under

skills/cinetic/scripts/test/. The working tools are 55 root scripts and 14 audio scripts.

  1. Two engines share one contract. Remotion 4.0.529 is the default.

HyperFrames 0.8.79 mirrors the tokens and the timeline contract. A reader must not assume that every script supports both.

  1. The version has an uncommitted section. CHANGELOG.md:9 starts an

[Unreleased] section above 1.5.1. A reader must not read the unreleased notes as part of the tagged version.

  1. The evaluation is not a win record. docs/evaluation.md:5 states that the

neutral blind judge was split: 7 wins in 23 comparisons. It also states that a cinetic run costs about three times the time and tokens of the baseline. The exploration must repeat both facts.

  1. Three of the seven briefs were never judged. docs/evaluation.md:15

states that briefs five, six, and seven were added after the judged rounds.

  1. The hard bans are not absolute. A supplied brand overrides a ban when the

brand file records the exception. The source shows this with the Quire paper-cream case in docs/evaluation.md.

  1. The 1.5.1 changelog entry is short. CHANGELOG.md:27 lists four

contributions and no metrics. The larger 1.5.0 entry holds the detailed claims.

  1. No runtime claim is verified. No film, gate, render, sound pass, or fetch

was run. Statements about output quality come from project documentation and source structure.

  1. The skill depends on a sibling project. earshot is a separate checkout in

this container. cinetic calls it but does not vendor it.

  1. The Python floor has two values. README.md:191 requires Python 3.11 or

later. README.md:205 and skills/cinetic/scripts/py.mjs:10-11 accept 3.10 or later. The source does not reconcile the two values.

  1. The copy deadline has two values. copy-check.py:2 says a stranger must

know the product within 5 seconds. copy-check.py:105 sets WHAT_BY_S = 3.0, and the check enforces 3 seconds.

  1. The interpreter diagnostics and the selector test different packages.

frames.mjs:50-53 tests numpy, scipy, soundfile and cv2. py.mjs prefers an interpreter with numpy, scipy and cv2. scripts/requirements.txt lists more packages than either check.

  1. The frame rate has two values. SKILL.md:196 states 30 frames per second.

The templates ship with FPS = 60, and the evaluation expects 60 when a brief gives no rate (docs/evaluation.md:187). The scaffold rewrites the template constant from --fps, whose default is 30. The source does not reconcile the contract and the evaluation.

  1. The second-round rule has two versions. SKILL.md:176 allows a second

round only after a gate fail. review-loop.md:73 also allows one after a fixed P0. The review guide is wider than the short path.

  1. The changelog count is off. CHANGELOG.md:27-31 says "Three community

contributions" and lists four changes. The entry credits pull requests 4, 8 and 3, and gives no metrics.

  1. The judged version is not the shipped version. docs/evaluation.md:124

and docs/evaluation.md:189 state that rounds 6 and 7 tested 1.0.0 snapshots. The fixes from those rounds shipped after the judged snapshot. No judged round covers the release of 1.0.0 or version 1.5.1.

  1. The fourth history weight has no stated use. bank.ts:154 seeds the deal

with the last three films. bank.ts:158 holds four weights, and bank.ts:168 can apply all four. The source does not say when the fourth value applies.

  1. The HyperFrames path is a mirror, not a peer. The source states that

HyperFrames mirrors the tokens and the timeline contract (SKILL.md:13). The starter has 13 files against 328 in the Remotion starter. Full feature parity is not established by the source.
