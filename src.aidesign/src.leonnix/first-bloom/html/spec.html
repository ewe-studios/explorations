first-bloom is a 30 second painterly nature film. A frost-covered pasque-flower bud opens on a frozen meadow. The spring that it releases runs across a mountain basin, thaws a creek and breaks a frozen waterfall into a double rainbow. From above, the basin is itself a six-petalled flower.

The tree holds 10,445 lines in src, 1,694 lines in audio, and 1,619 lines in the 19 root files. The three docs/ files are JPEG images. The world data and the audio files are not in the tree. bake.mjs builds the data. audio/fetch_audio.mjs downloads the library audio from its sources.

The repository has one commit. It adds the film, the render tools, the bake, the audio edit and the developer guide.

2. Scope

The exploration reads the source at the pinned commit. It explains the engine and its subframe accumulation, the post chain, the world layout and terrain bake, the bloom arrival field, the sky, the vegetation, the trees, the hero flower, the water and waterfall, the effects and birds, the camera timeline, the audio cue sheet, the offline render driver and the quality checks.

It does not run the bake, the render, the audio mix or the encode. It does not judge the visual or musical result. Where the source states a value, the exploration quotes the value and the line. Where the source does not state a value, the exploration says so.

The source gives several subframe counts for different stages. The engine default is 8. The final render command uses 16. The developer guide states 8 to 16. The concept document asks for 4 to 8 on the flight shots. The README states 10 to 32. The exploration quotes each count with its context and does not merge them into one value.

The waterfall values also conflict. layout.js declares a drop of 150 metres. The same file builds a lip at height 236 metres. The developer guide gives a base near 20 metres. The exploration states each value and the disagreement. It does not choose one.
