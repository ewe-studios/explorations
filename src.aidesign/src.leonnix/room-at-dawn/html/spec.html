room-at-dawn is a photoreal bedroom at first light, built completely from code. No model file, no image texture and no high-dynamic-range image (HDRI) is in the tree. The room is a live first-person walk in three.js. The same scene also renders a 45 second film at 3,840 by 2,160 pixels in high dynamic range 10 (HDR10, perceptual quantizer over BT.2020, 10-bit HEVC).

The tree holds 10,086 lines of JavaScript under src, 268 lines of JavaScript tooling (vite.config.js and the four .mjs tools), 14 lines of PowerShell, and seven JPEG frames in docs/media. The lighting bake is a 13.06 megabyte binary at public/bake/gi.bin. Two recorded audio files sit in public/audio.

2. Scope

The exploration reads the source at the pinned commit. It explains the room shell and its parts, the procedural geometry and texture helpers, the seeded random number generator, the central processing unit (CPU) path tracer and its workers, the per-vertex global illumination plumbing and its shader patch, the box-projected reflection capture, the high dynamic range render pipeline, the film timeline and its sub-frame accumulation, the live game and its adaptive resolution, the development server bridge to ffmpeg, and the four tools.

It does not run the development server, the bake, the film render or the encode. It does not judge the visual result. Where the source states a value, the exploration quotes the value and the line. Where the source does not state a value, the exploration says so.

The shot table holds seven entries: wake 8.5 seconds, sill 7.0, leaves 5.5, duvet 6.0, macro 5.5, desk 5.0 and room 7.5. Each shot stores two camera keys with position, target, field of view, focus point and aperture radius. The sub-frame count per shot ranges from 32 to 96.

Two exposure controls coexist. LOOK.exposure is 19 for the live view. Each shot has its own exp multiplier from 0.62 to 1.5. applyFilmCamera multiplies the two. The exploration states both values and does not merge them.

The bake file records 160 samples per point. The default in the driver is 128. The README example uses 256. The exploration quotes each number with its context.
