celld Documentation

celld: a self-hosted, distributed Durable Objects daemon (Rust). Runs a Cloudflare Workers application — Workers, Durable Objects, KV, Queues, D1, R2, Workflows, Cron Triggers, and static assets — on your own machines from the wrangler.json you already have. A Cargo workspace where crates/logic owns behavior and crates/celld is the effect executor and adapter host.
