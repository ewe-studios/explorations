## What Flipper Is

A feature flag is one question — "is this feature on for this actor right
now?" — answered the same way no matter where the answer is stored. You ask
with `Flipper.enabled?(:search, user)`. You turn things on with
`Flipper.enable`, and target specific actors, groups, percentages, or
expressions. The storage is a pluggable adapter (memory, ActiveRecord, Redis,
Sequel, Mongo, Moneta, Dalli, Rollout, an HTTP cloud, and more), and the whole
thing is layered: a DSL in front of a Feature, six stateless Gates that decide
the answer, an Adapter contract that persists gate values, and decorator
adapters that add memoization, instrumentation, strictness, and failover.
